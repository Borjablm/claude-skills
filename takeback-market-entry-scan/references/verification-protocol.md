# Verifying partners

## Evidence grades
| Grade | Counts as | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Primary, dated | Confirms | Company register filing, regulator permit register, the organisation's own dated news or report, a dated press release |
| Primary, undated | Exists only | The organisation's own live page with no dates. Never confirms "operating" |
| Secondary, dated | Lead, may confirm location or history | Trade press, local news, a network's member list |
| Directory or aggregator | Lead only | Business directories, AI-written listings |
| Model or search summary | Nothing | The search tool's or fetch tool's own summary, any AI answer |

## Order of checks
1. **Exists.** Company register (Companies House, CRO core.cro.ie, KvK, national registers, charity registers). Record the number and status.
2. **Operating.** Dated activity in the last 12 months: filings, news, social posts, tenders. Older than 12 months is a warning; older than 24 is a call question.
3. **Handles this material.** Dated evidence that it takes this product now. A 2019 article about a pilot is history, not capacity. Its absence is the most common gap and goes on the call list.
4. **Permits.** Look the organisation up on the regulator's public register for the activity and the waste codes.
5. **Terms.** Only the organisation's own documents or a call.

## Traps seen in testing
- **Taken-over domains.** Two reuse organisations named by the no-web run had old domains that now served unrelated content (a betting guide, a stock-trading blog). Check that the site is still the organisation's before citing it.
- **Names slightly wrong.** "Rediscover Centre" for the Rediscovery Centre; a Scottish waste firm placed in Northern Ireland.
- **Anonymous history.** The web run found a 2019 article about a carpet-tile reuse partner in Belfast that did not name the firm. It was the partner the 2019 team had signed. Search the trade press of the period for the scheme's name to recover names.
- **Verifier conflation.** A verifier read a later news article's date as the date of the agreement it reported. Keep "date of evidence" and "date of the event" apart.
- **Undated confirmations.** Verifiers marked undated pages as confirmed; the checker catches it. Downgrade, do not back-fill a date.

# Call list template

The desk scan ends here. Each question names who can answer it.

## Regulators (per jurisdiction)
- Which waste code do you expect for this product when it is collected separately for reuse? Does that change any levy?
- Can a partner clean, grade and resell it under an exemption or registration, or does it need a permit or licence? Which class, how long does it take?
- Is direct reuse (owner hands over tiles for certain reuse, no processing) outside waste law? What records do you want?
- For each border: green-list (Annex VII) or notification for clean, sorted loads? Who signs as notifier or arranger?
- Which route applies to storing and refurbishing the product: registration, a general binding rule, an exemption or a permit?
- Does a carrier registered in another nation or country need to register with you as well?

## Candidate reuse partners
- Do you take this product today? From whom, how much a month, what condition?
- What do you do with what you cannot resell?
- Which authorisations do you hold, and for which codes?
- Storage space, handling equipment, staff who could be trained, appetite for a written agreement.
- What would you need from the programme (pallets, collection, a fee, volume guarantee, marketing)?

## Logistics
- Can you collect palletised product from sites in the market, under which permit, and at what rate per pallet or per tonne?
- Back-haul options with the client's own deliveries.

## Recyclers outside the market
- Do you accept this product from this origin, which brands and backings, what spec, who pays freight?

## Competitors (from their own teams)
- Is the market actually served? Minimum volume, who pays, which brands accepted, certificate issued?

## Disposal sites (price baseline)
- Gate fee per tonne for this waste code, landfill and energy recovery, plus levy, plus typical skip or collection charge.

## Public buyers
- Next tender date, and how take-back will be scored.

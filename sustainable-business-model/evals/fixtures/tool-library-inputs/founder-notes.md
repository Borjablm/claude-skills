# Tool library — notes from the founding meeting, March 2027

We want to open a community tool library: people borrow drills, sanders, ladders
instead of buying them. Membership is 40 EUR a year. We start with one site.

Rough numbers from the pilot we ran in the community centre last autumn:
- We had about 120 members signed up in six months.
- Each member borrowed roughly 5 times a year.
- The tools cost us about 9,000 EUR to buy initially (donated half of them).
- Repair and replacement ran at maybe 15% of the tool value per year.
- The council might give us the space for free, we've asked but nothing signed.
- One part-time coordinator, 14,000 EUR a year for 20 hours a week.

We think we can get to 600 members by year three across two sites.

Environmental case: every borrow avoids a purchase. A drill is about 3.5 kg of
CO2e to manufacture according to the guidance we read.

# Call list: what to ask (the part of the scan a person does)

Send photos and a sample description before the call. Ask for the answers in writing afterwards.

## A recycler
1. Are you taking this material now, from outside your country? From outside the EU?
2. Which polymers do you accept, and which do you refuse? Do you need a lab test (FTIR, DSC) per lot?
3. What must be removed before it ships (lead lines, rings, floats, ropes, selvedge)? Any contamination limit by weight (sand, organics, other polymers, moisture)?
4. How do you want it packed (bale size and weight, cut length, big bags)? Minimum lot size? Do you want a sample first, and how big?
5. Do you pay, take it free, or charge a gate fee? On what basis (per tonne delivered, ex works)? Who pays freight?
6. How many tonnes a year could you take from us, and from when?
7. What happens to a load you reject (return, disposal, who pays)?
8. Will you act as consignee on a waste-shipment notification and sign the contract it requires? Is your facility pre-consented?
9. Who is the right person for future lots?

## A collector or aggregator
1. Do you take material from our origin, or only from your own network?
2. Where does each fraction go (name the recycler), and what spec do they impose on you?
3. What do you charge or pay, and who handles the shipment paperwork?

## A forwarder
1. Which carriers accept waste bookings on this lane, and what do they need before accepting the box?
2. Routing and transhipment ports (each non-EU transit authority may need to consent).
3. Quote for a 40 ft high cube, door to port and port to plant, including terminal handling and any waste surcharge.
4. Experience with notifications under Regulation (EU) 2024/1157? Can you act for the notifier?
5. Realistic tonnes per container for the packing you propose.

## The competent authorities
1. Destination authority: confirm the procedure and classification, the financial guarantee basis, and the fees.
2. Origin authority: export permit, who is the Basel focal point, how long consent takes.

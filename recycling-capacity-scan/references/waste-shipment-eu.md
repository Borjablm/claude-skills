# Shipping waste for recycling into or out of the EU

Checked against the text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1157 on EUR-Lex on 8 November 2026. Law changes: re-read the articles before relying on this, and confirm the procedure with the competent authority of destination.

## 1. Two questions decide the procedure
1. **Is the origin country covered by the OECD Decision?** (The OECD members plus a few others.) If not, is it a Party to the Basel Convention? Check the UN Treaty Collection, chapter XXVII-3, for the date and type (ratification or accession).
2. **What is the waste as it will actually be shipped?** Classify it after any stripping, sorting and baling done at origin, not as it sits on the quay.

## 2. Imports into the EU for recovery (Title V, Chapter 2)
| Origin | Rule | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| OECD Decision country | Allowed (Art. 52(1)(a)) | Art. 53: Title II applies with adaptations. Green-listed waste can move under the general information requirements (Art. 18, Annex VII); hazardous, Y48-type and unlisted waste need prior written notification and consent |
| Non-OECD country that is a Basel Party | Allowed (Art. 52(1)(b)) | **Art. 54 applies Art. 51: prior written notification and consent for every shipment, green-listed or not.** Written consent from the authorities of dispatch, destination and any transit; a contract with the consignee; a financial guarantee or equivalent insurance (Art. 51(4)) |
| Non-OECD, not a Basel Party | Only under a bilateral or multilateral agreement (Art. 52(1)(c) and (d)) | Per the agreement |

Practical points from Title II as applied:
- Authorities decide within 30 days of the notification being complete (Art. 9(1)).
- A consent is valid for at most one year (Art. 9(4)). A general notification covering several shipments to a pre-consented facility can run for three years (Art. 13(12)).
- Recovery must be completed within one year of receipt (Art. 9(6)).
- From 21 May 2026 notifications go through the EU's electronic system (Art. 27). A notifier outside the EU with no access may use post or signed email (Art. 51(2)(a)), and Annex IC keeps paper valid for third-country shipments without system access.

## 3. Exports out of the EU (for the reverse question)
Exports of plastic waste to non-OECD countries are restricted much more tightly (Title IV; Art. 39 and the plastic provisions), and new limits phase in between 2026 and 2027 (Art. 86(3)). Read the current text; do not reuse the import table above.

## 3a. United Kingdom (pointers only, not checked in detail)
Since leaving the EU, Great Britain applies its own retained version of the older Regulation (EC) No 1013/2006, and Northern Ireland follows EU rules for many waste shipments. Moves within the UK fall under domestic duty-of-care rules (registered waste carriers, waste transfer notes), not shipment notifications. Before planning a UK route, read the current guidance of the regulator for the origin: SEPA (Scotland), Environment Agency (England), Natural Resources Wales, NIEA (Northern Ireland), and the gov.uk pages on importing and exporting waste. Quote what you rely on. This file does not summarise those rules because they were not verified for it.

## 4. Basel plastic entries (in force since 1 January 2021)
- **B3011** (Annex IX): plastic waste presumed not hazardous. It covers nearly single-polymer, non-halogenated plastic, almost free from contamination and destined for environmentally sound recycling, plus PE/PP/PET mixtures recycled separately. Check the full annex text for whether your polymer is covered by the "including but not limited to" wording.
- **Y48** (Annex II): other plastic waste, including mixtures, unless hazardous. Basel prior informed consent applies.
- **A3210** (Annex VIII): plastic waste presumed hazardous.
- Lead weights, metal rings, antifouling coatings or oil can move a load out of B3011. Stripping at origin is what makes a load cleaner, and a cleaner load is easier to place with a buyer.

## 5. Codes for the paperwork
- European Waste Catalogue (Decision 2000/532): **02 01 04** waste plastics (except packaging) from agriculture, aquaculture, forestry, hunting and fishing; **19 12 04** plastic and rubber from the mechanical treatment of waste.
- Customs: HS heading 3915 (waste, parings and scrap of plastics). Confirm the subheading with the forwarder.

## 6. What only a person can settle
- Whether the destination authority accepts your classification.
- The financial guarantee amount, and the notification fees of each country involved.
- Which carriers accept waste bookings on the lane, and the actual freight quote.
- Who signs as notifier when the exporter is a small company at a remote port.
